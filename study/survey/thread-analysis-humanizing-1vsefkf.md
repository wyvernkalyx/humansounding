Permalink: https://www.reddit.com/r/humanizing/comments/1vsefkf/

## The finding that matters

**Rank 1 and rank 2 are the same company.**

- verva.com's own homepage says "TwainGPT is now Verva."
- verva.com publishes the rebrand announcement at
  https://verva.com/blog/introducing-verva, titled "TwainGPT Is Now Verva."

The post presents Verva (#1, "the best AI humanizer right now") and TwainGPT
(#2, "another solid choice") as two independent products that were separately
tested. They are one product and its former brand name. Rank 3, QuillBot, is the
only genuinely third-party entry, and it is the one that fails.

So the structure is: our product wins, our old name places, the outsider loses.

Confidence: high. Verified directly on the vendor's own site.

## The second finding

verva.com sells **both sides**. It runs `verva.com/ai-humanizer` and
`verva.com/ai-detector` from the same domain. That is the pattern from the
detector-landscape doc, now with a fourth name in it alongside Undetectable.ai,
TextGuardAI, and QuillBot.

It also runs a review farm on its own blog: "BypassGPT Review: The Worst AI
Humanizer?", "Smodin Review", and two separate positive reviews of TwainGPT,
which is itself. Competitor reviews published by a competitor.

Pricing, from the site: $8/mo for 8,000 words, $20/mo for 30,000, $40/mo
unlimited, all billed annually. Homepage claim, verbatim: "the most advanced,
consistent, and effective AI humanizer on the market," which is the
unfalsifiable-claim column filled in before we even test it.

Confidence: the Verva/TwainGPT identity is verified. The coordination reading is
inference from posting behaviour, and should be labelled that way if it is ever
published.

## Formatting note

The numbered list uses `1․` with U+2024 ONE DOT LEADER rather than a period, and
the #1 link is plain `http://`. Substituting a lookalike character for a period
is a known way to slip past automated filters. This may be an artifact of how
the text was copied, so treat it as an observation to re-check on the live page
rather than a finding.
