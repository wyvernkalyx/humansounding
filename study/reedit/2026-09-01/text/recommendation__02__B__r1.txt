**What changed and why:**

| First version (reader is new to the candidate) | This version (reader knows the background) |
|---|---|
| Opens by establishing your relationship and tenure | Opens by naming what you're uniquely positioned to add |
| Recounts the career trajectory and hire date | Assumes it; goes straight to the above-grade evidence |
| Lists achievements broadly | Selects the two or three that specifically predict success at the *next* level |
| Maps the job description requirement by requirement | Assumes the committee has the JD; addresses the likely *objection* instead |
| Growth area framed gently as coachability | Growth area named plainly, because a knowledgeable reader will raise it anyway and pre-empting it builds credibility |
| Formal, complete, ~500 words | Compressed, conversational, argumentative — a memo between peers |

The core principle: with an informed reader, description is noise. Everything that survives the edit should be either **evidence they don't have** (private moments of judgment, unassigned work, difficult conversations you witnessed) or **an argument** about how to interpret evidence they do have.
