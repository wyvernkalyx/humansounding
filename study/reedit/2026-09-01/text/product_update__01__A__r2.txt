# Dashboard redesign: shipped

**Live for all accounts today.** Biggest change from beta: filters are now global, not per-widget — set a date range or segment once and it applies everywhere.

**Other deltas from beta:**
- Layouts persist and sync across devices (they reset on logout in beta)
- Render time down to ~1s at 20+ widgets, from ~3s
- Shareable views added, including read-only external links
- Dark mode added

**Didn't make it:** custom widgets (beta next month), scheduled digests, metric alerts.

**Migration:** widgets carried over in order. Legacy dashboard available from the settings menu for 60 days, then retired.

**Known issues:** filters don't apply to embedded reports yet; layout sync can lag ~30s across open sessions.

Feedback to the header button or back to me.
