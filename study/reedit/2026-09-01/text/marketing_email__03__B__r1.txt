**What changed and why**

- **Assumed knowledge.** No definition of the provision, no "what this means for you," no reassurance that we'll skip the jargon. Jargon is the shared language here — avoiding it reads as condescension.
- **Value proposition inverted.** The first version sold clarity. This one sells the unresolved edges, competing interpretations, and exam experience — the things a practitioner can't get from reading the primary source themselves.
- **CPE moved up front.** For this reader it's often the deciding factor, and the eligibility mechanics matter.
- **Urgency dropped.** "Seats are limited," countdown pressure, and the enthusiastic P.S. tend to reduce credibility with a professional audience. The effective date supplies the urgency on its own.
- **Named panel with affiliations.** Peers evaluate the speaker before the topic. Firm names and any former agency background do more work than years of experience.
- **Deliverables reframed.** A citation-backed summary and a white-label client explainer are practice assets, not lead-magnet checklists.
