# Mobile Performance: The Fixes Are Live

**Release [X.Y] — [Date]**

The mobile slowdown is fixed. Details below.

## Cold start: 4.1s → 1.6s

We deferred non-essential initialization until after first interactive paint. Analytics, feature-flag hydration, and prefetch warming now run post-render instead of blocking it. Measured on the mid-tier Android reference device, not a flagship.

## Bundle: 38% smaller

Dropped four unused dependencies, moved to AVIF/WebP across the board, and code-split by route. You now pull down the screen you asked for, not the whole app.

## Scroll: locked at 60fps

List virtualization is in. Inbox, search results, and the activity feed recycle views rather than rendering the full set. The frame drops into the 30s on long lists are gone.

## Offline behavior

Broader cache coverage, exponential backoff on retries, and an action queue that flushes on reconnect. The indefinite spinner on flaky connections should no longer be reproducible — if you hit it, file it.

## Battery: −22%

Background sync batches network calls and honors Doze / Low Power Mode. Telemetry is from the beta cohort over two weeks, so treat it as directional until we have broader data.

## Benchmark target has moved

We now gate releases against the mid-tier Android device on simulated 3G. Flagship-on-Wi-Fi numbers are no longer sufficient to ship. This is the change most likely to affect your work — budget accordingly.
