Here's a tight version, main point first:

---

I'm now Director of Operations at [Company].

Same team, bigger lever. Thanks to everyone who helped get me here — more soon.

---

**Or, if you want one line of warmth:**

---

New title today: Director of Operations at [Company].

Grateful to [manager's name] and the team. The work ahead is the fun part.

---

**What I cut:**
- The "you already know the story" framing — at this length, skipping the recap is implied, not stated
- The multi-part thank-you, down to one clause
- Anything explaining the role or what comes next

One thing worth checking: where's this going? A Slack post or company newsletter blurb can stay this bare. If it's still LinkedIn, this reads a little clipped for the platform — people expect at least a sentence of substance. And if it's for a bio line or email signature, you'd want it stripped further to just the title and company, no announcement framing at all.
