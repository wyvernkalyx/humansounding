**Notes for customization:**

- **Swap the vague for the specific.** Naming one or two real changes in the opening lines will outperform generic "big changes are coming" language.
- **Segment if you can.** A version for business owners and a version for individuals will beat one email trying to serve both.
- **Timing.** Send 10–14 days out, with reminders at 3 days, 1 day, and 1 hour before.
- **Compliance check.** If you're a licensed firm, run the copy past whoever handles your advertising review — claims about savings or outcomes often need qualifying language.
