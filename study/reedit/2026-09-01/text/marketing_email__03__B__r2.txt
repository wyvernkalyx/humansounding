**Cuts made**

- **Lead is now the hook.** The unresolved mechanic plus the effective date opens the email — that single sentence carries the argument for attending, so it survives even if nothing below the fold gets read.
- **Agenda trimmed from five items to four,** each to one line. Documentation and exam experience folded out; they were valuable but the least differentiated.
- **Subject lines cut to one.** Shorter placements usually mean a single slot, and option 1 does the most work.
- **Panel reduced to two names,** keeping the firm affiliation and the agency background — the two credentials that move registration.
- **Disclaimer kept.** It's compressible but not droppable if your advertising review is involved.
