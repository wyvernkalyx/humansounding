Assuming a Slack/intranet post or short email blast — I've dropped the memo header, since those placements carry sender and date automatically. Add it back if you need the formal format.

---

**Security Awareness Training is due [Deadline].**

Complete it at [URL] → "[Course Name]." Takes ~[30–45] minutes, resumable, [80]% to pass with unlimited retakes.

New this cycle: [new module], [updated content], and a scenario-based assessment replacing multiple choice.

Manager completion reports go out [date]. Access restrictions apply after the deadline per [Policy #].

Exceptions or accommodations: [Name], [email].

---

What I cut and why:

- **Moved the deadline to the first line, as the headline.** In a scannable placement, the one thing that must survive a two-second glance is the date and the fact that action is required.
- **Merged access and logistics into one line.** Platform, time, and passing score are all "how," and a returning reader processes them as a single unit.
- **Compressed "what's new" from bullets to a sentence.** Bullets cost vertical space; three short items read fine inline.
- **Dropped "same platform, same process as last cycle."** Implied by the absence of any note to the contrary.
