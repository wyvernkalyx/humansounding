# Dashboard 2.0 — now live

Shipped to all accounts today. Here's what changed.

**Custom layouts.** Widgets are drag-and-drop and resizable. Layouts save automatically and sync across devices.

**Live metrics.** Refreshes in real time, not on the old five-minute cycle.

**Global filters.** Date range, segment, and team filters apply to every widget at once and persist between sessions.

**Load performance.** Rewrote the data-fetching layer. 20+ widget dashboards render in ~1s, down from ~8s.

**Saved views.** Save a configuration, share it with your team, or generate a read-only external link.

**Dark mode.**

## Migration

Your widgets carried over in their existing order — no action needed. **Reset to default layout** is in the settings menu if you'd rather rebuild.

Legacy dashboard stays available from the same menu for 60 days, then it's retired.

## Known gaps

Two things from the old dashboard didn't make this release: CSV export on individual widgets, and the annotation pins on time-series charts. Both are on deck for the next cycle. If either is load-bearing for you, hold off on switching or let us know.

## Next up

- **Custom widgets** — build charts from any workspace data (beta next month)
- **Scheduled digests** — dashboard delivered to email or Slack
- **Metric alerts** — threshold notifications on any number
