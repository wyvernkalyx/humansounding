## Build notes

**Segmentation** — Split the dormant pool at minimum three ways before build: activated-then-lapsed, onboarded-but-never-activated, and seat-holders who were never the buyer. The "what's changed" block only lands for group one; group two needs a setup path, group three needs routing to the account owner instead.

**Dynamic content** — `[Month]` and the feature block should pull from last-login timestamp and plan tier. If you can't populate features dynamically, cut to the plain-text variant rather than shipping generic release notes.

**Cadence** — Day 0 product-led, Day 5 customer story or use-case, Day 12 incentive with explicit last-touch framing, then sunset. Hold any discount to send 3.

**Suppression** — Exclude open support tickets, active trials, accounts in renewal windows, and anyone with a billing failure in the last 30 days (different journey entirely). Confirm the 90-day window measures last *login*, not last email open — those diverge badly for API-heavy accounts.

**Reply handling** — Send from a monitored address. The plain-text variant will generate replies; route them somewhere before launch or drop the CTA.

**Measurement** — Primary metric is 30-day return-to-active, not opens or clicks. Secondary: unsubscribe rate as a list-hygiene signal, and reply volume as qualitative churn data. Expect the plain-text version to win on replies and lose on clicks.
