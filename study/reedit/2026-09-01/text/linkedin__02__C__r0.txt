Here's a draft you can adapt:

---

I'm excited to share that I've been promoted to Director of Operations at [Company]!

When I joined [X years] ago as [previous role], I couldn't have predicted how much I'd learn — or how much I'd come to care about the people I get to work alongside every day.

Thank you to [manager's name] for the trust and the coaching, and to my team for making the hard days worth it. Every improvement we've made has been a group effort, and none of it happens without people willing to question how things have always been done.

I'm looking forward to what's ahead: [one sentence on a goal or focus area — scaling the team, improving processes, entering new markets, etc.].

Onward. 🚀

---

**A few things to personalize:**
- Swap in a specific accomplishment or two if you want more substance ("helped cut fulfillment times by 30%")
- If your workplace culture is more understated, cut the emoji and the "Onward"
- Tagging your manager and a few colleagues tends to boost reach — and it's a nice gesture

Want a shorter version, or one with a different tone (more humble, more celebratory, more story-driven)?
