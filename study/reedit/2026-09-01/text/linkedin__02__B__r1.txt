**What changed and why:**
- Cut the "when I joined X years ago" arc — that's exposition for strangers
- Dropped the explanation of what the role means
- Traded specific accomplishments for knowing references ("you know which projects") — an insider nod reads as confidence, not vagueness
- Shortened overall; readers who know the context get impatient with setup

One caveat worth naming: LinkedIn is a mixed audience by default. Even if your close network knows everything, recruiters, former colleagues, and second-degree connections will see this too. If reaching them matters to you, the first version does more work. If this post is really for your immediate circle, the short one lands better and sounds less like a press release.
