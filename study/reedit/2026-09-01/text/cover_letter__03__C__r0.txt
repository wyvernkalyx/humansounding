## Tips for customizing this

- **Replace vague claims with specifics.** "Built a full-stack app" is weaker than "built a Django app that lets users track workouts, with 200 lines of unit tests and CI via GitHub Actions."
- **Mirror the job posting's language.** If they say "React and TypeScript," those exact words should appear if they're true for you.
- **Keep it to one page** — roughly 250–400 words. Hiring managers skim.
- **Address a real person** when you can. Check LinkedIn or the company's team page; "Dear Hiring Team" is fine as a fallback, but avoid "To Whom It May Concern."
- **Lead with enthusiasm for their work, not just your need for a job.** One sentence proving you researched the company beats three paragraphs about yourself.
- **Don't apologize for inexperience.** Skip phrases like "although I lack professional experience." Frame what you *have* done instead.
