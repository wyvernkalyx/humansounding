# Mobile performance — v[X.Y] release notes

**[Date]**

Startup, scroll, and battery work from the last two cycles has landed. Numbers below are P50 on a Pixel 6a over simulated 3G unless noted.

## Changes

**Cold start: 4.1s → 1.6s.** Moved analytics init, feature-flag hydration, and font preloading off the critical path. First screen is interactive before the deferred work runs; if you were relying on flags being resolved at mount, they now resolve on first paint instead — check any consumers that read the flag store synchronously.

**Bundle: −38% on disk.** Dropped four unused dependencies (list in the changelog), moved to AVIF with WebP fallback, and route-split the JS bundle. Per-screen chunks are fetched on navigation, so the first hit to a rarely-used screen is ~120ms slower on cold cache. Prefetching lands next cycle and closes that gap.

**List rendering: sustained 60fps.** Inbox, search, and activity feed now use view recycling. Previously 30–40fps on mid-tier hardware with lists over ~200 items. Custom row heights are supported but require an explicit estimate — omitting it falls back to measure-on-scroll and you'll lose most of the gain.

**Network resilience.** Cache TTLs extended, retries moved to exponential backoff with jitter, and offline actions now queue to a local write log and replay on reconnect. Conflict resolution is last-write-wins for now; field-level merge is on the roadmap.

**Energy use: −22% per typical session.** Background sync batches calls into a single wake window and honors Doze / Low Power Mode. Sync intervals stretch under battery saver — expect staleness up to 15 minutes in that state rather than the usual 2.

## Benchmarking
