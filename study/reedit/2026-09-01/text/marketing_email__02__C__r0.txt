**A few notes on customizing this:**

- **Swap the specifics.** The details — your neighborhood, your class names, your hours — are what make it feel local rather than like a chain-gym blast.
- **Add scarcity if it's true.** "Only 20 spots in our January starter program" converts well, but only use it if it's genuine.
- **Include a photo** of your actual space or members, not stock imagery.
- **Send timing:** Dec 28–Jan 3 catches peak motivation; a follow-up around Jan 15 catches people whose resolution already wobbled.
