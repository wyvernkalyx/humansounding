# Mobile, but faster

**Product Update — [Date]**

We've spent the last two release cycles focused on a single question: why does the app feel slower on a phone than it does on a laptop? Today's release is the answer to that question — and the fix.

## What changed

**Faster cold starts.** We rebuilt how the app loads on launch, deferring non-essential work until after the first screen is interactive. Cold start time is down from 4.1s to 1.6s on mid-tier Android devices.

**Smaller download, smaller footprint.** We removed unused dependencies, switched to modern image formats, and split our JavaScript bundle so you only download the code for the screen you're actually looking at. The app is now 38% smaller on disk.

**Smoother scrolling.** Long lists — your inbox, search results, activity feed — now recycle views instead of rendering everything at once. Scrolling holds a steady 60fps on devices where it previously dropped into the 30s.

**Better offline and low-signal behavior.** We cache more aggressively and retry failed requests intelligently instead of leaving you staring at a spinner. Actions taken offline now queue and sync when you reconnect.

**Less battery drain.** Background sync now batches network calls and respects system power-saving modes. Early telemetry shows a 22% reduction in energy use during typical sessions.

## Why it matters

More than half of our sessions happen on mobile, and a growing share of those are on older devices and slower networks. Performance isn't a nice-to-have there — it's the difference between finishing a task and abandoning it. We've moved our internal targets to reflect that: every release is now benchmarked against a mid-tier Android device on a simulated 3G connection, not a flagship phone on office Wi-Fi.

## What's next

We're not done. Next up:

- Predictive prefetching so common navigation paths feel instant
- Faster image loading with progressive placeholders
- Reducing time-to-interactive on the three heaviest screens in the app
