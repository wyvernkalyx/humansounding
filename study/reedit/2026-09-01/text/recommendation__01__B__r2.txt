**What went and why:**

- **Verdict moved to the first sentence.** Everything after it now functions as support rather than buildup.
- **Section headers cut.** At this length they'd outnumber the content.
- **Transcript defense dropped** — it's the least load-bearing item, and a reader with the file can ask.
- **One anecdote kept, told faster.** The replication story does more work than the other three points combined, so it survives at nearly full length while everything around it compresses.
- **Caveat and fit advice merged** into a single practical paragraph, since both answer the same question: how do we place this person?

If you need it shorter still, the CV-correction paragraph is the next to go.
