# Re-Engagement Email: 90-Day Inactive Customers

## Subject Line Options
1. It's been a while, [First Name] — here's what you've missed
2. Your [Product] account is still here (and it's gotten better)
3. We saved your spot
4. 3 things that changed since your last visit
5. Still interested? Just checking. 👀

**Preview text:** Your account, settings, and data are exactly where you left them.

---

## Main Email

**Subject:** It's been a while, [First Name] — here's what you've missed

Hi [First Name],

We noticed you haven't logged into [Product Name] in a few months. No hard feelings — life gets busy.

But a lot has changed since [Month of last login], and we think a few things might be worth a second look:

**[Feature/Update #1]** — [One-line benefit, e.g., "Reports that used to take 20 minutes now build themselves."]

**[Feature/Update #2]** — [One-line benefit]

**[Feature/Update #3]** — [One-line benefit]

Your account is exactly as you left it — [settings, saved data, integrations, team members] all intact. One click and you're back in.

[**Log back in →**]

If [Product Name] isn't the right fit anymore, we'd genuinely like to know why. Just hit reply — a real person reads every response, and it takes 30 seconds to tell us what went wrong.

Thanks for giving us a shot in the first place,

[Sender Name]
[Title], [Company]

*P.S. — [Optional incentive: "Come back this week and we'll extend your plan by 30 days, on us." or "Want a 15-minute walkthrough of what's new? Grab a time here."]*

---

## Shorter Alternative (Plain-Text Style)

**Subject:** Still interested?

Hi [First Name],

Quick one: you haven't logged into [Product Name] since [Month]. I'm trying to figure out if that's because:

1. You're busy and just haven't gotten back to it
2. Something wasn't working the way you expected
3. You've moved on to something else

If it's #1 — [your account's right here]. If it's #2 or #3, hit reply and tell me. I'd rather hear it than guess.

[Sender Name]
[Company]

---

## Notes for Deployment

- **Personalize the "what's new" section** by segment. A dormant power user needs different reasons to return than someone who never finished onboarding.
- **Lead with value, not guilt.** Avoid "We miss you!" framing — it centers your needs, not theirs.
- **Save the discount for email 2 or 3.** Leading with a coupon trains customers to churn for deals and devalues the product-led reason to return.
- **Suggested cadence:** Day 0 (this email) → Day 5 (social proof / customer story) → Day 12 (incentive + clear last-chance framing) → sunset from the active list.
- **Include a visible unsubscribe and a "pause emails" option.** Cleaning your list improves deliverability more than squeezing a few extra sends out of unengaged contacts.
- **A/B test the subject line first** — it drives the majority of open-rate variance in win-back campaigns.
