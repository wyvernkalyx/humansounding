**A few tips as you customize:**

- **Replace the bullets with your real numbers.** Even modest metrics ("grew Instagram engagement 18%") beat vague claims. If you don't have numbers, describe scope instead: "managed content for a 12,000-person subscriber list."
- **The mission paragraph matters more here than in corporate applications.** Nonprofits screen for people who won't leave in eight months. Be specific and honest rather than effusive.
- **Find a real name if you can** — check the org's staff page or LinkedIn. "Dear Hiring Committee" is a fine fallback; avoid "To Whom It May Concern."
- **Mirror their language.** If the posting says "storytelling" or "constituent engagement," use those words.
- **Keep it to one page** — around 300–400 words is plenty.
