Notes on this version:

- **It's riskier than the first draft.** Naming month six and taking public responsibility is the thing that makes it credible to people who were there — a vaguer version would read as spin, and they'd know. But if there's any active dispute about who decided what, or if leadership above you sees this as airing internal business, run it past someone first.
- **Watch for it landing as blame.** Lines like "several of you tried" are meant to credit people, but a reader who raised a flag and got overruled may read it as being volunteered into your redemption arc. Worth checking with one or two of them.
- **I cut the closing question.** Inviting an audience who lived the failure to share their own failures reads as tone-deaf.
- **Length:** this is close to the ceiling for a post where the reader has no story pulling them through. If you trim, cut the second takeaway before the first — the discovery-ratio point is the one that changes future behavior.
