## Third Option — Data-Led

**Subject:** [X] [items] processed while you were away

[First Name],

Since [last login date], [Account Name] has:

- [Metric 1 — e.g., processed 1,240 tickets]
- [Metric 2 — e.g., saved an estimated 31 hours]
- [Metric 3 — e.g., flagged 8 exceptions nobody reviewed]

That last one's the reason I'm writing. [Specific consequence of the unreviewed items.]

[**Review flagged items →**]

[Sender Name]

---

## Notes

- The behavioral merge fields (last project, teammate activity, unreviewed items) are what make these work. Without live data feeding them, fall back to the shorter version — a vague "we miss you" performs worse than a two-line note.
- The third option only sends to accounts with genuine accrued activity. Firing it at a dormant single-seat account with zero data reads as automated and undermines the other two.
- Third option assumes multi-seat or ongoing automation; skip it for solo, manual-use accounts.

---

*Assumed "knows the background" means the recipient — an existing user with account history — so I've cut the product framing and written these to reference their specific configuration and usage rather than re-explaining the tool. Say the word if you meant something else and I'll adjust.*
