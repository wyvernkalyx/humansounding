**Overview**

I'm writing to share that we are reorganizing the Customer Service team, effective **[Month Day, Year]**. This change moves us from a single generalist support pool to three specialized teams. Our goal is to shorten resolution times, deepen expertise, and give every team member a clearer path for growth.

**Why We're Making This Change**

Over the past year, our ticket volume grew 38% and our product line expanded to include [Product/Service]. Under the current model, agents handle everything from password resets to complex billing disputes, which has led to longer handle times, more transfers, and uneven customer outcomes. Feedback from the Q[X] engagement survey also told us that many of you want more focused work and clearer advancement opportunities. This reorganization responds directly to both.

**What's Changing**

Beginning [Date], the department will be organized into three teams:

- **Tier 1 Support** — First-contact resolution for account access, order status, and general inquiries. Led by [Name].
- **Technical Support** — Product troubleshooting, integrations, and escalated technical cases. Led by [Name].
- **Billing & Retention** — Invoicing, refunds, plan changes, and cancellation saves. Led by [Name].

A new **Quality & Enablement** function, led by [Name], will own training, coaching, and QA across all three teams.

**What This Means for You**

- **No positions are being eliminated.** Every current team member has a role in the new structure.
- **Compensation and benefits are unchanged.** Pay, PTO balances, and tenure are unaffected.
- **Team assignments** were based on demonstrated strengths, past performance, and the preferences many of you shared in your last one-on-one. Your manager will confirm your assignment individually by **[Date]**.
- **Some reporting lines will change.** If your manager is changing, you'll meet with your new manager before the transition date.
