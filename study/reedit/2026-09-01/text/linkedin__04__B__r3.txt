**Everyone believes in hiring for attitude. Almost nobody has built the machinery to do it.**

The gap isn't conviction — it's assessment and capacity.

**Assessment:** if your attitude signal lives in the unstructured chat at the end, you're measuring rapport, not attitude. Put it in the scorecard with defined anchors, or it collapses into "people like me." And probe failure, not success — anyone can narrate a win. Listen for where the agency sits when the story goes badly.

**Capacity:** a high-potential hire with a skills gap is a real cost — to your seniors, your dates, and to the hire themselves if nobody has bandwidth to coach.

One flag: if "attitude over experience" keeps producing candidates who are junior, cheap, and conveniently agreeable, that's not a philosophy. That's a budget.

Where does it break for you — the assessment, or the support after the offer?
