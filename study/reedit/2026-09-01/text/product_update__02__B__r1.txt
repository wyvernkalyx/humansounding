**Sync behavior.** Real-time webhooks replace [15-minute] polling. Two-way sync means [Tool] can now write back to [Product] — if you were relying on [Product] being authoritative, review your conflict settings before activating.

### What changed and why

- **Cut the rationale entirely.** A reader who knows the background doesn't need the [v1 API] deprecation story again; repeating it reads as filler or defensiveness.
- **Cut the feature list.** They've seen the pitch. What they haven't seen is the breakage detail.
- **Promoted the breaking changes** from a one-line FAQ answer to the longest section — for an informed reader this is the actual payload of the post.
- **Added "known gaps."** A general audience gets reassurance ("we've mapped most workflows"); an informed audience gets the specific list, because they'll find out anyway and would rather find out now.
- **Timeline became a status board** rather than a schedule — checkmarks tell a returning reader what they've already lived through.

If you meant a different "already knows" — internal engineering, the support team prepping for ticket volume, or a partner-facing note to [Tool] themselves — the emphasis shifts again. Happy to take a pass at any of those.
