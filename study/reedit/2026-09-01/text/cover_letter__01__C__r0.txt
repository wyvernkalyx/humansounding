- Mirror 2–3 keywords from the job posting (e.g., "3PL," "yard management," "DOT compliance," "SLA adherence").
- Keep it to one page. If you're over ~400 words, cut the weakest bullet.
- If you're changing industries, add one sentence early on connecting your background to logistics-specific challenges.
