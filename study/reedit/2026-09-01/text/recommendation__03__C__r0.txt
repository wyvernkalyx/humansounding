## Summary

[Name] delivered an outstanding year of revenue performance, finishing at **127% of annual quota ($2.54M against a $2.0M target)** and ranking second on a team of nine. He consistently wins competitive deals, builds genuine trust with buyers, and is the person teammates seek out when a negotiation stalls.

That commercial strength is offset by persistent gaps in administrative execution — CRM hygiene, deal documentation, expense reporting, and forecast submissions. These are not cosmetic issues: they have created downstream problems for forecasting accuracy, deal desk turnaround, and his own commission processing. Closing this gap is the single highest-leverage change [Name] can make in the coming year, both for the team and for his own advancement.

## Key Accomplishments

- **Quota attainment:** 127% of plan; four of four quarters at or above target.
- **New logo acquisition:** Closed 14 new accounts, including [Account A] ($310K ACV), the largest new-business win in the region this year.
- **Win rate:** 34% on qualified opportunities, against a team average of 26%.
- **Expansion:** Grew [Account B] from $45K to $128K through a well-executed multi-threading strategy that he later shared in a team enablement session.
- **Customer feedback:** Named specifically in three customer reference calls as a reason the buyer chose us. Post-sale CSAT on his accounts averaged 4.7/5.
- **Team contribution:** Volunteered to mentor two new hires during ramp; both attained quota in their first full quarter.

**Strengths observed:** Discovery and qualification, executive presence, competitive positioning, resilience after losses, willingness to help peers.

## Development Area: Administrative Execution and CRM Discipline

Specific observations from the review period:

- **Forecast submissions:** Late in 9 of 12 months, typically by 2–4 days. On three occasions, missing data required [Manager] to reconstruct pipeline figures manually before the regional roll-up.
- **CRM data quality:** Opportunity close dates and next steps were stale in approximately 40% of open deals at any given time (team average: 12%). Two deals closed in the quarter without contract values updated, which distorted Q3 forecast accuracy by roughly $180K.
- **Deal documentation:** Incomplete handoff notes on five closed-won accounts required Customer Success to re-interview the customer on scope and commitments. One instance created a misalignment on implementation timeline that took three weeks to resolve.
- **Expense and commission paperwork:** Q2 commission payment was delayed by one pay cycle due to unsubmitted deal approval forms. Expense reports were submitted an average of 41 days after incurrence, against a 30-day policy.

**Why this matters:** Revenue results are excellent, but the surrounding process is how the business plans headcount, recognizes revenue, and serves customers after the sale. Right now, [Name]'s administrative gaps are being absorbed by his manager, deal desk, and CS partners. That is not sustainable, and it is currently the primary barrier to consideration for a Senior AE or team lead role, both of which carry responsibility for modeling process discipline for others.

## Agreed Action Plan

2. **Manager check-in:** Weekly 1:1s will open with a five-minute pipeline hygiene review for the first 90 days, then move to biweekly if metrics hold.
3. **Tooling support:** [Name] will complete the mobile CRM logging training in January and enable automated activity capture, which should reduce manual entry substantially.
4. **Administrative support:** Requesting approval for [Name] to use [X hours/month] of sales support resource for proposal formatting and document assembly — a reasonable investment given his revenue leverage. Note: this supports the workflow but does not transfer ownership of forecast accuracy or CRM data.
5. **90-day review point:** Progress on Goals 2–5 will be formally reviewed on [Date]. Sustained improvement over two consecutive quarters is the stated prerequisite for Senior AE consideration.
