# Dashboard 2.0 is live — and 8x faster

Rebuilt from the ground up. 20+ widget dashboards now load in ~1s, down from ~8s.

**Also new:**
- Drag-and-drop, resizable widgets that sync across devices
- Real-time metrics (no more five-minute cycle)
- Global filters that persist between sessions
- Saved views with shareable read-only links
- Dark mode

**Migration:** Your widgets carried over as-is. Legacy dashboard stays available in settings for 60 days.

**Not in this release:** per-widget CSV export and time-series annotations. Both are coming next cycle.

Feedback button is in the header.
