## Build notes

**Segment first.** Activated-then-lapsed, onboarded-but-never-activated, and non-buyer seat-holders need different sends. This copy fits group one.

**Suppress** open tickets, active trials, renewal windows, and recent billing failures.

**Confirm** the 90-day trigger measures last login, not last open.

**Route replies** to a monitored inbox before launch, or cut the CTA.

**Cadence:** Day 0 this send → Day 5 use-case → Day 12 incentive, last touch → sunset.

**Primary metric:** 30-day return-to-active. Not opens.
