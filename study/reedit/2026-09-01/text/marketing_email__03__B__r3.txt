**What I removed — and a flag**

I read the results claim as **"defensible readings"** and **"the exposure profile of each."** Both imply a prediction about how a position would fare if challenged, which is the kind of outcome assertion advertising review usually objects to. Replacements are neutral: "competing readings," and "the considerations bearing on each" describes the analysis without forecasting how it lands.

Everything else is unchanged.

**Worth confirming:** if what you actually needed cut was something else, tell me which line and I'll swap it instead. Two other candidates, in case:

- **"how firms are taking positions in the meantime"** — arguably implies the approaches discussed are working in practice. Neutral alternative: *"how firms are approaching the open items."*
- **"client-ready explainer you can rebrand"** — a deliverable promise rather than a results claim, but some reviewers treat "client-ready" as an implied fitness-for-use assurance. Neutral alternative: *"a draft client explainer you can adapt."*
