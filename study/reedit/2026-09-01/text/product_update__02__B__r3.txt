# Classic [Tool] integration shuts off [Sunset Date]

**Migrate now → [migration guide]**

Read-only mode starts **[Date]**. At sunset, Classic connections are deleted and syncing stops. Data in [Product] and [Tool] is unaffected — but nothing moves between them until you reconnect.

**To migrate:** [Settings → Integrations → [Tool] → Connect]. Needs [admin] in [Tool]. Field mappings import automatically — verify, activate, disconnect Classic.
