### Tips for Customizing This Letter:
1. **Highlight Oversight vs. Execution:** Board members advise and govern rather than execute. Ensure the examples you highlight focus on *guidance, oversight, risk assessment,* and *strategy* rather than day-to-day management tasks.
2. **Align with the Board’s Needs:** If you know the specific board matrix gap they are trying to fill (e.g., Audit Committee financial expert, cybersecurity literacy, international growth), make sure that keyword appears clearly in the second paragraph.
