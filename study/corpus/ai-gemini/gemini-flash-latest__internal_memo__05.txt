***

**MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** All Employees  
**FROM:** [Your Name/Department, e.g., People & Culture Team]  
**DATE:** October 24, 2023  
**SUBJECT:** Updates to Our Annual Performance Review Process  

---

### **1. Purpose**
As part of our commitment to continuous growth, transparency, and employee development, we are updating our Annual Performance Review process for the upcoming cycle. 

Based on employee feedback from our recent engagement survey, our goal is to shift away from a backward-looking evaluation toward a more continuous, forward-focused conversation about career development, achievements, and goal alignment.

---

### **2. What Is Changing?**

Here is a summary of the key updates taking effect this cycle:

* **Shift to Forward-Looking Development:** Rather than focusing solely on past performance, discussions will dedicate equal time to professional development goals, skill-building, and future career aspirations.
* **Simplified Evaluation Criteria:** We have streamlined our core competencies from [e.g., 10 categories to 4 core pillars] to make self-evaluations and manager reviews clearer and less time-consuming.
* **Streamlined Rating Scale:** We are replacing the previous [e.g., 1–5 numerical scale] with a four-tier descriptive model: *Exceeding Expectations, Meeting Expectations, Approaching Expectations,* and *Needs Improvement.*
* **Incorporation of 360° Peer Feedback:** Employees will now have the option to invite up to [e.g., two peers] to provide brief, constructive feedback to ensure a well-rounded review.
* **Quarterly Check-Ins:** The annual review will no longer be a standalone event. Starting next quarter, we will introduce lightweight, quarterly 1-on-1 check-ins to track progress and adjust goals in real time.
