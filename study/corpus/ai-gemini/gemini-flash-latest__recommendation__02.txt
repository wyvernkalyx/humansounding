Here is a customizable template for an internal promotion recommendation letter. Fill in the bracketed information with specific details.

***

**[Date]**

**To:** [Hiring Manager's Name or Search Committee]  
**From:** [Your Name, Your Current Title]  
**Subject:** Recommendation for [Employee’s Name] – [Title of the Role They Are Applying For]

Dear [Hiring Manager’s Name / Search Committee],

I am writing to give my highest recommendation to [Employee's Name] for the position of [Target Job Title] within our [Target Department/Team Name] department. As [Employee’s Name]’s [manager/supervisor/colleague] for the past [number] [months/years] in their role as [Employee’s Current Title], I have witnessed firsthand their exceptional work ethic, strategic thinking, and dedication to our company’s mission.

During their time in [Current Department], [Employee's Name] has consistently exceeded performance expectations. One of their most notable achievements was [briefly describe a major accomplishment, e.g., leading the rollout of the X project, streamlining the Y process, or increasing team revenue/efficiency by X%]. They have a proven track record of delivering high-quality results on time and under budget. 

What makes [Employee’s Name] uniquely qualified for this promotion is their readiness to take on advanced responsibilities. Beyond mastering their current duties, they have actively demonstrated leadership by [give 1–2 examples, e.g., mentoring junior team members, spearheading cross-functional initiatives, or proposing innovative solutions to recurring problems]. 

Furthermore, [Employee’s Name] already possesses a deep understanding of our company culture, internal workflows, and strategic goals. This institutional knowledge means they will have a virtually seamless transition into this new role, requiring minimal ramp-up time compared to an external hire. They are universally respected by their peers and regularly collaborate effectively across different teams.

While our team will certainly miss [Employee’s Name]’s direct contributions, I am genuinely excited about the value they will bring to the [Target Department/Role]. I have no doubt they will excel as [Target Job Title] and continue to drive positive impact for our organization.

Please feel free to reach out to me directly at [Your Extension/Phone Number] or [Your Email] if you would like to discuss [Employee's Name]’s qualifications further.

Sincerely,

[Your Signature]

**[Your Name]**  
[Your Title]  
[Your Department]  
[Your Contact Information]

***

### Tips for Customizing this Letter:
1. **Focus on Next-Level Skills:** Highlight abilities that the *new* role requires (e.g., if it’s a management role, emphasize their mentorship and project management, not just their technical execution).
2. **Use Concrete Metrics:** Instead of saying "they improved efficiency," try "they automated our weekly reporting, saving the team 5 hours per week."
3. **Emphasize Institutional Value:** Remind the hiring manager that an internal hire saves time, training costs, and onboarding friction.
