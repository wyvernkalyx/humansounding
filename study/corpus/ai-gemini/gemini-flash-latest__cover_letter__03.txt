### 💡 Quick Tips for Customizing This Letter:
1. **Match the Tech Stack:** Look at the job description and make sure the programming languages and frameworks mentioned in the letter directly align with what they are asking for.
2. **Highlight One Strong Project:** If you lack professional experience, treat a personal or capstone project like a job. Focus on the *problem it solved* and *technologies used*.
3. **Include Links:** Make sure your GitHub and Portfolio links are clickable and active. Junior developer candidates who provide clean code samples immediately stand out.
