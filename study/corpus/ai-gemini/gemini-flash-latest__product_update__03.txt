Here are two templates for a product update about improved mobile performance—one for a **blog post / email newsletter** and another for a **short in-app message / App Store release note**.

---

### Option 1: Blog Post / Email Newsletter (Detailed & Engaging)

**Subject:** ⚡ [Product Name] just got a major speed boost on mobile

**Headline:** Faster, Smoother, Better: Experience the New [Product Name] on Mobile

We know that work doesn’t just happen at your desk. Whether you’re reviewing updates between meetings, checking metrics on your commute, or managing projects from the field, you need a mobile experience that keeps up with you.

Over the past few months, our engineering team has been hard at work under the hood. Today, we’re excited to roll out our **biggest mobile performance update yet**.

Here is what’s new:

*   **⚡ 2x Faster Load Times:** We’ve optimized our core architecture, cutting screen load times by up to **[e.g., 50%]**. Your dashboards and feeds now open almost instantly.
*   **🧈 Silky-Smooth Navigation:** Say goodbye to stutter. Scrolling, searching, and switching between tabs are now fluid and responsive, even on older devices.
*   **🔋 Lighter on Battery & Data:** We’ve reduced background processing and optimized media caching, meaning the app now uses **[e.g., 30%] less battery and significantly less mobile data**.
*   **📶 Better Offline & Low-Connectivity Support:** Weak signal? No problem. The app now handles spotty network connections gracefully, saving your actions and syncing automatically once you’re back online.

**How to get the update:**
The performance improvements are live today on both iOS and Android. If your apps don't update automatically, simply head to the **[App Store / Google Play Store]** to download version **[X.X]**.

👉 **[Update Your App Now]** *(Button)*

We’re committed to making [Product Name] the fastest, most reliable tool in your pocket. Try it out and let us know what you think by replying to this email or dropping a review in the app store!

— **The [Product Name] Team**

We’ve completely rebuilt our mobile engine to make your experience faster and smoother than ever.

* **Smoother Scrolling:** Eliminated lag across all screens and feeds.

Update now to experience the difference!

### 💡 Tips for customizing this:
* **Add real metrics:** If you have real benchmarks (e.g., *“Time to interactive dropped from 3.2s to 1.1s”*), include them—data builds trust.
* **Acknowledge user feedback:** If performance was a common customer complaint, add a line like: *“You told us the app felt sluggish, and we listened.”*
