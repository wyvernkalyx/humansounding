### 💡 Tips for Customizing This Email:
* **Segment your list:** If you serve both individuals and small business owners, tailor the bullet points (e.g., mention *Section 179/depreciation* for businesses, or *child tax credits/standard deduction* for individuals).
* **Include the Speaker's Credibility:** If your host is a CPA, CFP, or former IRS agent, add a quick 1-sentence bio (e.g., *"Hosted by Jane Doe, Senior CPA with 15+ years of experience"*).
* **Automate Reminders:** Set up an automated reminder email for **24 hours before** and **1 hour before** the webinar to increase attendance rates.
