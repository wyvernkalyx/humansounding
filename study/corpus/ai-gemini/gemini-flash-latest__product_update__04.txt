Here are three templates for announcing Single Sign-On (SSO) support, depending on where you plan to share the update.

---

### Option 1: Product Blog Post / Customer Email (Comprehensive)

**Subject:** New Feature: Single Sign-On (SSO) is now available in [Product Name] 🔐

**Headline:** Level Up Your Security with Single Sign-On (SSO)

Managing multiple passwords shouldn’t get in the way of getting work done. We’re excited to announce that **Single Sign-On (SSO)** is now officially available on [Product Name]! 

With SSO, your team can now access [Product Name] securely using your organization’s existing identity provider—no more forgotten passwords, reset requests, or security bottlenecks.

#### Why you’ll love SSO:
* **Enhanced Security:** Reduce the risk of compromised credentials by enforcing your organization’s password policies, multi-factor authentication (MFA), and session rules.
* **Frictionless Logins:** Team members can log in with a single click using the tools they already use every day.
* **Centralized User Management:** IT administrators can easily manage user access, onboarding, and offboarding from a single dashboard.

#### Supported Identity Providers
Our integration supports **SAML 2.0**, allowing seamless connection with top identity providers, including:
* Okta
* Microsoft Azure AD / Entra ID
* Google Workspace
* OneLogin
* PingIdentity (and any standard SAML 2.0 provider)

#### Availability & Setup
SSO is available starting today for all customers on our **[Enterprise / Business Plan]**.
