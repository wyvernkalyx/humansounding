### 💡 Tips for Customization:
1. **ERP Software:** Mid-sized manufacturers care heavily about the systems you know. Be sure to replace the placeholder with the exact ERP you have used (e.g., Epicor, NetSuite, SAP Business One, Microsoft Dynamics 365, Sage).
2. **Quantify Results:** Wherever you see **[X]%** or **[Number]**, add actual metrics from your past experience (e.g., *“shortened close by 2 days,” “managed $15M in inventory,” “improved accuracy to 99%”*).
3. **Bridge the Gap:** If you have spent time on the shop floor talking to shift supervisors or production managers, keep the sentence about *“partnering with plant managers”*—hiring managers love accountants who don't just stay behind a desk.
