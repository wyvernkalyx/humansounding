It’s not wrong, but it’s too generic. “Results-driven professional” is a common résumé phrase, and “10+ years of experience” doesn’t tell the reader what you actually do, what you’re good at, or what results you produce.

A stronger structure is:

“[Role/specialty] with 10+ years of experience in [key areas], with a track record of [specific result or strength].”

For example: “Operations leader with 10+ years of experience improving processes, managing cross-functional teams, and reducing costs in high-growth organizations.”
