You can be supportive of your friend without endorsing the business or risking your money. Keep the answer about your investment decision rather than trying to convince her that the business will fail.

You could say:

“Thanks for thinking of me and for sharing the business with me. I’ve thought about it, and I’m going to pass on investing. I don’t feel confident enough in the opportunity to put money into it, and I’d rather keep our friendship separate from a financial investment. I’m rooting for you, though, and I hope it goes well.”

If she asks what concerns you, you can be more specific without being harsh: “I have some concerns about the demand/business model/financial projections, so it isn’t an investment I’m comfortable making.”

You don’t need to defend the decision beyond that. If she keeps pushing, “I appreciate that you feel strongly about it, but I’ve made my decision and won’t be investing” is enough.
