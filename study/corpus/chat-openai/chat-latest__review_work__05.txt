It’s clear, but very generic. “Founded in 2019,” “team of,” “passionate,” and “experts” are all common About-page language, so the opening doesn’t tell visitors what makes you distinctive.

You’ll usually get a stronger opener by leading with what you do, who you do it for, or why you exist. For example:

- “Since 2019, we’ve helped independent retailers turn complex inventory data into better buying decisions.”
- “We build cybersecurity tools for small teams that don’t have dedicated security staff.”
- “We started in 2019 with a simple idea: getting mental healthcare shouldn’t require weeks of phone calls.”

The founding year can still be useful, but it’s rarely the most compelling first fact. If you tell me what the company does and the tone you want, I can rewrite the opening.
