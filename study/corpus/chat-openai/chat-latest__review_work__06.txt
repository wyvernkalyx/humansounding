Yes. “Circle back” is common and acceptable in work emails, especially in business settings. It’s somewhat corporate/jargony, though, so a more direct phrase can often sound cleaner.

For example:
- “I’ll circle back with you next week.” → “I’ll follow up with you next week.”
- “Can we circle back on this tomorrow?” → “Can we revisit this tomorrow?”
- “Just circling back on my last email.” → “I’m following up on my last email.”

If your workplace has a casual corporate tone, “circle back” is perfectly normal. For formal, international, or especially concise communication, “follow up” or “revisit” is usually better.
