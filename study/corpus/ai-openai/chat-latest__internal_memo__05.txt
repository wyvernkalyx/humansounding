Subject: Updated Annual Review Process

We are updating our annual review process to make performance discussions more timely, consistent, and focused on employee development. The revised process will take effect for the upcoming review cycle.

Key changes include:

- More frequent check-ins: Managers and employees will have regular performance conversations throughout the year rather than relying primarily on the annual review.
- Updated evaluation criteria: Reviews will use clearer, role-relevant criteria covering results, skills, collaboration, and progress toward established goals.
- Employee self-assessments: Employees will complete a brief self-assessment before the formal review to highlight accomplishments, challenges, and development priorities.
- Greater focus on development: Reviews will include specific goals and development plans for the coming year, including opportunities to build skills and expand responsibilities.
- Standardized timelines: All teams will follow a common review schedule to improve consistency across the organization.

Managers will receive guidance and training on the updated process before the review cycle begins. Employees will also receive instructions, key dates, and access to any required forms or systems.

These changes are intended to make performance reviews more useful, transparent, and connected to ongoing work and professional growth. Questions about the new process can be directed to [HR contact/email].
