Experience can get someone through the door. Attitude often determines what happens next.

Skills can be taught. Processes can be learned. Industry knowledge can be built. But curiosity, accountability, resilience, and a willingness to learn are much harder to develop.

That doesn’t mean experience doesn’t matter. For some roles, it’s essential. But when two candidates can both do the job, I’d pay close attention to the person who:

- Asks thoughtful questions
- Takes ownership of mistakes
- Seeks and applies feedback
- Adapts when circumstances change
- Makes the people around them better

A résumé tells you what someone has done. Their attitude can tell you what they’re capable of doing next.

Hire for potential, not just proof.
