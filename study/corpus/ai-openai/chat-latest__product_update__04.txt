Single sign-on (SSO) is here

We’ve added SSO support to make signing in faster, simpler, and more secure for your organization.

With SSO, team members can access their accounts using your organization’s existing identity provider, reducing password management and helping IT teams manage access centrally.

What’s included:
- Sign in through your organization’s identity provider
- Centralized user access and authentication
- A streamlined login experience for your team
- Easier onboarding and offboarding

Admins can configure SSO from the organization settings. Once enabled, team members can use their existing work credentials to sign in.

Check out our SSO setup guide to get started.
