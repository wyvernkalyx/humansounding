Introducing the Redesigned Dashboard

We’ve redesigned the dashboard to make it faster and easier to find what matters most. The new experience brings clearer navigation, a more focused layout, and key information to the forefront—so you can spend less time searching and more time getting things done.

What’s new:
- Simplified navigation for quicker access to the tools you use most.
- A cleaner layout that makes important metrics and activity easier to scan.
- Improved performance for faster loading and smoother interactions.
- Better responsive design across desktop, tablet, and mobile.

The redesigned dashboard is rolling out now. We’ll continue refining the experience based on your feedback, so let us know what you think.
