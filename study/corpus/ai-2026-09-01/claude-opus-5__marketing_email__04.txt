**Body:**

Hi [First Name],

It's been about three months since you last logged in to [Product], so we wanted to check in — no strings attached.

If [Product] wasn't the right fit, that's genuinely useful for us to know. But if life just got busy, here's what you've missed:

- **[New feature]** — [one-line benefit, e.g., "cuts your weekly reporting down to about five minutes"]
- **[Improvement]** — [one-line benefit]
- **[Content or integration]** — [one-line benefit]

Your account and data are exactly where you left them.

**[Log back in →]**

Not sure where to start? Reply to this email and [Name] from our team will point you to the one thing most likely to be useful for [use case / role]. A real reply, from a real person.

Talk soon,
[Sender name]
[Title], [Company]

*P.S. Would rather hear less from us? [Update your preferences] or [unsubscribe] — no hard feelings.*

---

**Optional incentive variant** (swap in after the bullet list if you're offering one):

> To make coming back easy, here's [X% off your next month / an extended trial / 500 bonus credits], yours through [date]. Just log in and it's applied automatically.

---

**A few notes on making this work:**

- **Lead with what changed, not with guilt.** "We miss you" underperforms concrete news about the product.
- **One CTA.** The reply option is a soft secondary path, not a competing button.
- **Segment if you can.** Users who onboarded but stalled need different copy than users who never activated — the first group needs a reason to return, the second needs help getting started.
- **Keep the unsubscribe visible.** Re-engagement sends have elevated spam-complaint risk, and complaints hurt deliverability for your whole list. Making opt-out easy protects your sender reputation.
- **Send at 3–4 emails max**, spaced 5–7 days apart, then sunset non-openers from your active list.
