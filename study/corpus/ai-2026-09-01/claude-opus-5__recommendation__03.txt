**What happened:**

- Forecast submissions were late in **[#] of [#]** cycles, requiring follow-up from me or Sales Ops each time.

- Two deals ([Account A], [Account B]) experienced processing delays of **[#] days** due to incomplete order forms or missing approvals at submission.

**Why it matters:**

2. **Forecast integrity.** Leadership makes hiring, inventory, and investment decisions off the forecast. Stale pipeline data from a high-volume rep distorts the whole picture.
3. **Team cost.** Sales Ops estimates **[#] hours** of rework this year attributable to incomplete submissions — time not spent supporting the rest of the team.
4. **Personal risk.** Undocumented deals are fragile. If [Name] is out sick, on PTO, or moves roles, the institutional knowledge leaves with them.

**Context I want to acknowledge:** Part of this is a real workload issue. [Name] carried [#] accounts, [%] above the team average, and admin burden scales with deal volume.
