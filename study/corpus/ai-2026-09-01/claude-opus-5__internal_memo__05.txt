## Purpose

Beginning with the [Year] cycle, we are making four changes to how we conduct performance reviews. This memo outlines what is changing, why, and what you need to do.

## Why We're Making These Changes

Feedback from last year's engagement survey and manager focus groups pointed to three consistent problems: reviews arrived too late to be useful, ratings felt disconnected from day-to-day work, and compensation discussions overshadowed conversations about growth. The changes below address each of those issues directly.

## What Is Changing

**1. Quarterly check-ins replace the mid-year review.**
Instead of one formal mid-year conversation, managers and employees will hold documented check-ins at the end of each quarter. These are intended to be brief — 30 minutes — and focused on progress against goals, obstacles, and support needed. The annual review will summarize these conversations rather than introduce new feedback.

**2. Compensation decisions are separated from development conversations.**
Performance reviews will be conducted in [Month]. Compensation and promotion decisions will be communicated in [Month + 2]. This separation is intended to let the review conversation focus on growth without the distraction of a pending salary number. Reviews will still inform compensation decisions; the timing is simply decoupled.

**3. The rating scale is changing from five levels to four.**
The new scale removes the ambiguous middle category that previously absorbed roughly 70% of all ratings:

| Rating | Definition |
|---|---|
| Exceptional | Consistently exceeded expectations and expanded scope |
| Strong | Met all expectations and exceeded several |
| Developing | Met most expectations; specific gaps identified |
| Not Meeting | Significant gaps requiring a documented improvement plan |

We are also discontinuing forced distribution. Managers will no longer be required to fit their teams to a predetermined curve. Calibration sessions will continue to ensure consistency across departments.

**4. Peer feedback becomes structured and limited.**
Each employee will select three peers, and their manager may add up to two. Peer feedback will be collected through a standardized four-question form and shared with the employee in themed summary rather than verbatim. Unsolicited peer input outside this process will not be included in reviews.

## What Is Not Changing

The review period remains [Month] through [Month]. Promotion eligibility criteria are unchanged. Employees on performance improvement plans continue under their existing terms.
