### Tips for customizing this
- **Replace every bracket**, and swap my example bullets for your real wins — numbers matter more than adjectives in ops hiring.
- **Name the company's actual priorities.** Check their careers page, press releases, or the job posting's first three bullets, and mirror that language.
- **Match the job description's scope.** If the role is 3PL warehousing, lead with WMS, labor management, and throughput. If it's transportation, lead with carrier management, DOT/FMCSA compliance, and cost per mile.
- **Mention systems by name** (SAP, Manhattan, Oracle WMS, McLeod, Excel/SQL, Power BI) — these are often keyword-screened.
- **Keep it to one page.** If it runs long, cut the third paragraph before cutting a bullet.
