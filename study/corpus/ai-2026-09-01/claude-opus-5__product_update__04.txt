# Single Sign-On (SSO) is here

**[Product Name] now supports SAML 2.0 and OIDC single sign-on**, so your team can log in with the identity provider you already use — and you can stop managing passwords one account at a time.

---

## What's new

**Log in with your existing IdP.** Users authenticate through your identity provider and land directly in [Product Name]. No separate password to create, rotate, or forget.

**Works with your stack.** We support any SAML 2.0 or OIDC-compliant provider, with tested setup guides for:

- Okta
- Microsoft Entra ID (formerly Azure AD)
- Google Workspace
- OneLogin
- JumpCloud
- Ping Identity

**Just-in-time (JIT) provisioning.** New users get an account automatically the first time they sign in — no manual invites, no waiting on an admin.

**Role mapping.** Map IdP groups to [Product Name] roles so people get the right permissions from day one, and lose them automatically when their group membership changes.

**Enforced SSO.** Optionally require SSO for everyone in your workspace, disabling password and magic-link logins so there's exactly one way in.

**Domain claiming.** Verify your email domain to make sure every account created with it routes through your IdP.

---

## Why it matters

- **Faster onboarding and offboarding.** Deactivating someone in your IdP removes their access here too — immediately.
- **Fewer credentials to attack.** No standalone passwords means no reused or phished passwords.
- **Centralized policy.** Your MFA, conditional access, and session rules now apply to [Product Name] automatically.
- **Cleaner audits.** Access decisions live in one system, with a single log to review.

---

## How to turn it on

1. Go to **Settings → Security → Single Sign-On**.
2. Choose **SAML** or **OIDC** and copy the ACS URL and Entity ID into your identity provider.
3. Paste your IdP metadata URL (or upload the XML) back into [Product Name].
4. Test the connection with your own account.
5. Flip on **Require SSO** when you're ready to enforce it org-wide.

Most teams finish in under 15 minutes. Full walkthroughs live in the [SSO documentation](#), including provider-specific guides and a troubleshooting checklist.
