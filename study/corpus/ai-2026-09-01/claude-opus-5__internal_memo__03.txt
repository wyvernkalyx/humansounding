**Purpose**

I'm writing to share details of an upcoming reorganization of our Customer Service team, which will take effect on **[Date]**. This memo outlines what is changing, why we're making these changes, and what you can expect over the coming weeks.

**Why We're Making This Change**

Over the past [18 months], our customer base has grown by roughly [X]%, and the mix of issues we handle has shifted significantly toward [technical support / account and billing inquiries]. Our current generalist structure has served us well, but it has led to longer resolution times on complex cases and inconsistent handoffs between shifts.

The new structure is designed to:

- Reduce average resolution time by routing issues to specialized teams
- Create clearer escalation paths and ownership of complex cases
- Give team members deeper expertise and more defined growth tracks

**What's Changing**

Effective [Date], the Customer Service team will be organized into three specialized pods:

1. **Frontline Support** — Handles first-contact inquiries across all channels (phone, email, chat). Led by [Name].
2. **Technical Resolution** — Owns escalated technical issues and complex troubleshooting. Led by [Name].
3. **Accounts & Billing** — Manages billing disputes, subscription changes, and account administration. Led by [Name].

Each pod will operate with dedicated queues and its own performance metrics. A new **Escalation Desk**, staffed on a rotating basis, will manage handoffs between pods to ensure no customer is passed back and forth.

**What This Means for You**

- **Roles and reporting lines:** Every team member will be assigned to one of the three pods. Your manager will meet with you individually between [Date] and [Date] to discuss your assignment.
- **Compensation and job security:** This reorganization does **not** involve any reduction in headcount. No positions are being eliminated, and no one's compensation or benefits will change as a result of this restructuring.
- **Training:** Pod-specific training will run from [Date] to [Date]. Schedules will be distributed by [Date], and training hours are fully paid and count toward your regular schedule.
- **Shifts:** Current shift assignments remain unchanged for now. Any future adjustments will be discussed with at least [two weeks'] notice.

Thank you for the work you do every day. The changes described here are a direct response to the growth you've helped create, and I'm confident this structure will make your work more focused and more rewarding.
