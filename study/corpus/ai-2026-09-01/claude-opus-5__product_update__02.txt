**A few notes on adapting this:**

- **Give real lead time.** 60–90 days is typical; 30 is the floor if the integration is load-bearing for customers' workflows.
- **Be specific about "why."** Vague reasons ("to focus on our core experience") read as evasive. A concrete cause builds trust.
- **Segment the message** if you can — customers actively using the integration should get a direct email, not just a changelog entry.
- **Say what happens to existing data** explicitly. It's the first question people ask.
- **Don't over-apologize.** One honest acknowledgment at the end lands better than hedging throughout.
