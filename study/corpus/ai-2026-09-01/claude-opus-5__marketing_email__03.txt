**A few notes on customizing this:**

- **Swap the vague benefits for specific ones.** "The three changes to [specific credit] that affect anyone with [situation]" will outperform generic bullets significantly.
- **Name the pain.** If there's a hard deadline or a change with real dollar consequences, put that in the subject line and first sentence.
- **Establish credibility fast.** One line on why your presenter is worth 45 minutes ("has advised 200+ [industry] clients through [past change]") helps conversion.
- **Send 2–3 reminders:** one week out, one day out, and one hour before. The day-before and hour-before emails typically drive the majority of attendance.
