**What's changing**

1. **Single submission platform.** All expenses must be submitted through [System Name]. Emailed spreadsheets and paper forms will no longer be accepted after [Effective Date].

4. **Pre-approval requirements.** Written manager approval is now required in advance for:

   - Any single expense over **$500**
   - Client entertainment over **$150**
   - New software or subscription purchases of any amount
5. **Per diem for meals.** Travel meals will be reimbursed at a flat per diem of **$[XX]/day** for domestic travel and **$[XX]/day** for international travel, replacing itemized meal reimbursement.
6. **Reimbursement timing.** Approved expenses will be paid out on the **1st and 15th** of each month via direct deposit.

**What's not changing**

- Mileage continues to be reimbursed at the current IRS standard rate.
- Company card holders should continue reconciling transactions monthly.
- The list of non-reimbursable items (personal entertainment, traffic fines, upgrades to premium travel classes without approval, etc.) remains as previously published.
