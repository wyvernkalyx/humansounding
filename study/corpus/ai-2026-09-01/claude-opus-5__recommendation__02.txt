**Tips for customizing:**
- **Be specific and quantify where possible.** "Improved customer satisfaction scores from 82% to 91%" carries far more weight than "is great with customers."
- **Mirror the job posting.** Pull two or three key requirements from the internal listing and address them directly.
- **Include one honest development note.** Internal readers know the candidate; a letter with zero balance reads as boilerplate and loses credibility.
- **Keep it to one page.** Three to four tight paragraphs beat two pages of adjectives.
