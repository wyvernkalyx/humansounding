**Notes for tailoring:**

- **Name a real reason for this company.** Mid-sized manufacturers receive plenty of generic applications. One specific sentence about their products, markets, or growth separates you immediately.
- **Match their vocabulary.** If the posting says "cost accounting" or "absorption variances" or "BOM maintenance," use those exact terms — many firms screen with keyword filters.
- **Quantify at least two bullets.** Dollar amounts, day counts, and percentages carry far more weight than duty descriptions.
- **Name the ERP.** Manufacturers care a great deal about system fit. If you don't know theirs, mention the systems you know and note that you pick up new platforms quickly.
- **Keep it to one page.** Cut a bullet before you cut white space.
