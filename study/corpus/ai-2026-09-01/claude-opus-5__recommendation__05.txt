**A few notes on making this land:**

- **Specifics beat adjectives.** One concrete story about a hard decision is worth ten sentences of praise. Replace at least two of the bracketed examples with real detail.
- **Address the board's actual needs.** If you know what gap the committee is trying to fill (cyber risk, succession planning, sector expertise), name it directly.
- **Be honest about your vantage point.** Committees weigh recommendations from people who have seen the candidate in high-stakes settings far more heavily than warm general endorsements.
