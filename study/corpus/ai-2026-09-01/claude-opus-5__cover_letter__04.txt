### Quick customization notes

1. **Replace every bracket with real numbers.** Search committees read dozens of letters; specific data is what separates candidates.
2. **Name the school's own language.** Pull a phrase from their strategic plan, mission statement, or recent school board presentation and echo it in paragraph one or four.
3. **Match the role.** For a *principal* post, lead with vision, staff development, and budget/operations. For an *assistant principal* post, emphasize discipline systems, attendance, scheduling, and supporting a principal's agenda. For a *curriculum director* role, foreground standards alignment, assessment design, and PD facilitation.
4. **Keep it to one page** (this draft fits if you trim the bullet list to three items).
5. **Address a real person** whenever you can find the name—check the district website or call the HR office.
