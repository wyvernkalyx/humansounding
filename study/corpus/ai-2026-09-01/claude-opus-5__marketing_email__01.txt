**Notes for adapting it:**

- **Lead with the pain, not the feature.** The first two lines should describe a moment your user recognizes.
- **Three benefits max.** More than that and people skim past all of them.
- **One primary CTA.** The P.S. demo link is secondary — it catches people who aren't ready to click the main button.
- **Swap the stat** for a real beta number, a customer quote, or cut it. Vague stats hurt more than they help.
