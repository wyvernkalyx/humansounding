**Memo**

**To:** All Employees  
**From:** Human Resources  
**Date:** August 19, 2026  
**Subject:** Changes to the Annual Review Process

We are updating our annual review process to make performance conversations more timely, transparent, and development-focused. These changes are intended to better support employee growth, strengthen manager feedback, and align individual goals with team and organizational priorities.

### Key Changes

Beginning with the upcoming review cycle, the annual review process will include the following updates:

1. **Mid-Year Check-In**
   - In addition to the year-end review, employees and managers will participate in a formal mid-year check-in.
   - This conversation will focus on progress toward goals, challenges, support needed, and any adjustments to priorities.

2. **Updated Goal-Setting Process**
   - Employees and managers will set goals at the beginning of the review period.
   - Goals should be specific, measurable, and aligned with department and company objectives.
   - Goals may be revisited during the year as business needs change.

3. **Greater Emphasis on Ongoing Feedback**
   - Managers are encouraged to provide regular feedback throughout the year rather than waiting for the annual review.
   - Employees are also encouraged to seek feedback and discuss performance, development, and career interests on an ongoing basis.

4. **Self-Assessment Requirement**
   - Employees will complete a self-assessment before the year-end review.
   - The self-assessment will provide an opportunity to reflect on accomplishments, challenges, goal progress, and professional development.

5. **Simplified Rating System**
   - The rating structure has been revised to make evaluations clearer and more consistent across teams.
   - Managers will receive guidance to help ensure ratings are applied fairly and consistently.

6. **Development Planning**
   - Each annual review will include a development plan outlining skills to strengthen, growth opportunities, and potential training or support.
   - The goal is to ensure that performance reviews are not only evaluative but also forward-looking.

### Timeline

The updated review process will follow this general schedule:

- **Goal Setting:** Beginning of the review cycle  
- **Mid-Year Check-In:** Midpoint of the review cycle  
- **Self-Assessment:** Prior to year-end review discussions  
- **Manager Review:** End of the review cycle  
- **Review Conversation and Development Planning:** End of the review cycle

Specific deadlines and instructions will be shared by Human Resources before each stage of the process.

### Manager Responsibilities

Managers are expected to:

- Communicate expectations clearly.
- Provide timely and constructive feedback.
- Document key performance discussions.
- Support employee development and goal achievement.
- Complete reviews by the established deadlines.

### Employee Responsibilities

Employees are expected to:

- Participate actively in goal setting and review conversations.
- Track progress and accomplishments throughout the year.
- Complete the required self-assessment.
- Seek feedback and identify development needs.
- Work with managers to create meaningful development plans.

### Next Steps

Human Resources will provide additional guidance, including updated review forms, timelines, and training resources. Managers will receive training on the revised process before the next review cycle begins.

We appreciate everyone’s participation and cooperation as we implement these improvements. Our goal is to create a review process that is more useful, consistent, and supportive of employee success.
