## Product Update: Introducing the Redesigned Dashboard

We’re excited to introduce our redesigned dashboard — built to help you find key insights faster, navigate more easily, and stay focused on what matters most.

### What’s new

**A cleaner, more intuitive layout**  
We’ve reorganized the dashboard to make important information easier to scan at a glance. Key metrics, recent activity, and priority items are now more clearly grouped and accessible.

**Improved navigation**  
The updated structure makes it simpler to move between sections, reducing clicks and helping you get to the right information faster.

**Enhanced data visibility**  
Charts, summaries, and performance indicators have been refreshed for better readability, so you can quickly understand trends and take action with confidence.

**More responsive experience**  
The new dashboard is optimized across devices, giving you a smoother experience whether you’re working from a desktop, tablet, or mobile device.

### Why we made this change

As our product has grown, so has the amount of information available in the dashboard. This redesign is focused on reducing complexity, improving clarity, and making everyday workflows more efficient.

### When it’s available

The redesigned dashboard is rolling out starting today. You’ll see the updated experience the next time you log in.

We’re excited for you to try it and would love to hear your feedback as we continue improving the experience.
