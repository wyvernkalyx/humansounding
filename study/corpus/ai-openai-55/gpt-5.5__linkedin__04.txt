Experience matters — but attitude compounds.

I’d rather hire someone who is curious, accountable, coachable, and resilient than someone who simply “checks every box” on a job description.

Skills can be taught. Tools can be learned. Industry knowledge can be built.

But the way someone responds to feedback, handles uncertainty, supports teammates, and takes ownership? That often determines whether they grow — and whether the team grows with them.

Some of the best hires don’t come in with the longest résumé. They come in with:
- A willingness to learn  
- The humility to ask questions  
- The drive to solve problems  
- The maturity to own mistakes  
- The optimism to keep moving forward  

Experience may get someone in the door.

Attitude often determines how far they’ll go.

When hiring, don’t just ask: “Have they done this before?”

Ask: “Do they have the mindset to become great at it?”

That’s where the real potential lives.
