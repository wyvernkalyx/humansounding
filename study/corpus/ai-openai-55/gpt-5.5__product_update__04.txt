## Product Update: Single Sign-On is Now Available

We’re excited to announce support for **Single Sign-On (SSO)**, making it easier and more secure for teams to access our platform.

With SSO, users can sign in using their existing company credentials through an identity provider such as Okta, Google Workspace, Microsoft Entra ID, OneLogin, or any SAML 2.0-compatible provider.

### What’s new

- **Centralized authentication** through your organization’s identity provider
- **Improved security** with fewer passwords to manage
- **Faster onboarding and offboarding** for employees
- **Support for SAML 2.0-based login**
- **Optional enforcement of SSO** for your workspace

### Why it matters

SSO helps IT and security teams simplify access management while giving users a smoother login experience. By connecting authentication to your existing identity provider, admins can better control who has access and reduce the risk associated with password-based logins.

### How to get started

Workspace admins can configure SSO from the **Security** or **Authentication** settings page. Once set up, users will be able to log in using your organization’s identity provider.

If you’d like help enabling SSO for your workspace, contact our support team or your account manager.
