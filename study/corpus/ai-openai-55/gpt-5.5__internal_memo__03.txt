**Subject:** Customer Service Team Reorganization

We are announcing a reorganization of the Customer Service team to better support our customers, improve response times, and create clearer ownership across our service functions.

Effective **[insert effective date]**, the team will be organized into the following areas:

- **Frontline Support:** Responsible for handling initial customer inquiries, resolving common issues, and ensuring a timely first response.
- **Escalations and Resolution:** Focused on complex cases, priority issues, and cross-functional coordination to bring customer concerns to closure.
- **Customer Success Support:** Dedicated to proactive outreach, account support, and helping customers get the most value from our products and services.
- **Operations and Quality:** Responsible for reporting, process improvement, training support, and quality assurance.

As part of this change, some reporting relationships and responsibilities may shift. Managers will meet with their teams individually to review any role-specific updates, expectations, and transition plans.

This reorganization is intended to:

1. Improve the customer experience through faster and more consistent support.
2. Clarify roles and accountability within the team.
3. Strengthen collaboration between customer service and other departments.
4. Create more opportunities for employee development and specialization.

We recognize that change can raise questions, and we are committed to making this transition as smooth as possible. Over the coming days, leadership will share additional details, including updated team assignments, process changes, and timelines.

Thank you for your continued dedication to our customers and to one another. Your work is essential to our success, and we are confident that this new structure will help us serve our customers even more effectively.
