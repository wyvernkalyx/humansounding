## Product Update: Improved Mobile Performance

We’ve made significant improvements to the mobile experience to help the app feel faster, smoother, and more reliable on phones and tablets.

### What’s improved
- **Faster load times:** Key screens now open more quickly, especially on slower connections.
- **Smoother navigation:** Transitions between pages and actions feel more responsive.
- **Reduced data usage:** We optimized how content loads to use less bandwidth where possible.
- **Better stability:** We fixed several mobile-specific issues that could cause freezing or unexpected behavior.
- **Improved performance on older devices:** The app should now run more consistently across a wider range of mobile hardware.

### Why it matters
These updates are designed to make everyday mobile use faster and more dependable, whether you’re checking in on the go, completing tasks, or switching between workflows.

The improvements are available now. Make sure you’re using the latest version of the app or refresh your browser to get the best experience.
