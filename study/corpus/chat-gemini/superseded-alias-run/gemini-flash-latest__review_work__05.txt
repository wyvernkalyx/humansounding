To be completely honest: **it’s the most cliché, forgettable opening line in corporate copy.** 

Almost every B2B and agency website uses some variation of this sentence, which means it instantly washes over visitors without making an impact.

Here is why it doesn't work, along with how to fix it:

---

### Why it falls flat:

1. **"Founded in 2019"**: Unless your founding date carries heavy prestige (e.g., *“Brewing beer since 1740”*) or is central to a dramatic founding story, nobody cares *when* you started yet. 
2. **"Passionate"**: This is a classic "show, don't tell" word. Every company claims to be passionate. Prove passion through your work, case studies, and clear point of view—don't just state it.
3. **"Experts"**: Unearned authority. The word has lost its meaning because anyone can call themselves an expert.
4. **It’s about *you*, not the *customer***: The counter-intuitive secret of a great "About" page is that it’s actually about your customer, your mission, and the problem you solve for them.

---

### A Better Formula

A strong opening hook usually answers one (or more) of these three questions:
* **The Problem:** What broken thing in your industry did you set out to fix?
* **The Mission:** Who do you help, and what outcome do you create for them?
* **The Contrast:** How do you do things differently from everyone else?

---

### Examples of how to rewrite it:

* **If you want to be direct & value-driven:**
  > *"We help [target audience] achieve [specific big result]—without [common pain point]."*

* **If you want to keep the origin story, but make it punchy:**
  > *"We started in 2019 because we were tired of how [industry standard/annoyance] worked. So we built something better."*

* **If you want to focus on expertise (by showing, not telling):**
  > *"Since 2019, we’ve helped over [X] companies fix [specific problem]. Here’s why we do it differently."*
