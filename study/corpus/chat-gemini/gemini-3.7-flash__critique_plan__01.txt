In the meantime, migrating **400 users over a 48-hour window** is a "Big Bang" cutover. When migrations of this size fail or cause chaos on Monday morning, it is usually because one of the following areas was missed:

---

### 1. Data & Throughput Constraints
* **Did you pre-stage (pre-sync) the data?** 
  * You cannot sync 400 mailboxes (often multiple terabytes) from scratch in 48 hours due to API throttling (especially from Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, or on-prem Exchange).
  * *Standard fix:* Pre-sync 95%+ of historical data 1–2 weeks before cutover, then only run a **delta sync** over the weekend for recent changes.

---

### 2. DNS & Routing
* **TTL (Time to Live) Reduction:** 
  * Did you lower your MX, SPF, and Autodiscover DNS TTLs to **300 seconds (5 minutes)** at least 48–72 hours *before* the weekend? If not, old mail servers will still receive email well into Monday.
* **Mail flow lockdown:** Do you have a plan to disable inbound mail on the legacy system post-cutover to avoid split-brain delivery?

---

### 3. Identity, Access & Devices (The "Monday 8:00 AM" Blockers)
* **MFA / 2FA Enrollment:** Will 400 users be forced to set up MFA simultaneously on Monday morning? (This overwhelms help desks).
* **Mobile Devices:** Mobile devices (iOS Mail, Android, Outlook Mobile) rarely update automatically; they usually require users to delete the old account and add the new one.
* **Outlook Profiles:** Are you using a deployment script/tool (like ProfileUnity, BitTitan DeploymentPro, or GWSMO) to reconfigure desktop Outlook, or expecting users to create a new profile manually?
* **Autocomplete / NK2 Caches:** If users rely heavily on Outlook autocomplete, moving to a new system can cause bounce-backs (NDRs) if X500 addresses or legacyExchangeDNs aren't mapped.

---

### 4. Non-User Mailboxes & Hidden Dependencies
* **Shared Mailboxes & Calendars:** Are delegated permissions and send-as/send-on-behalf rights mapped and validated?
* **Printers, Scanners, and Copiers:** Do office multi-function printers rely on the old email server for "Scan-to-Email"?
* **Line-of-Business Apps:** Do your ERP, CRM, HR system, website forms, or monitoring tools use SMTP relay through the old email system?

---

### 5. Support & Rollback Planning
* **Help Desk Ratio:** On Monday, expect a 20–30% ticket rate (80–120 tickets for 400 users) if end-user steps are required. Do you have dedicated "floor walkers" or extra tier-1 support scheduled?
* **Rollback Trigger Point:** At what specific hour on Sunday do you make the "Go / No-Go" decision to revert DNS if data sync fails?
