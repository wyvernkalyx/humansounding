It is better **not to send that exact phrasing**, as it can make you sound disorganized or overwhelmed, even if it's the truth. 

Saying *"things have been crazy here"* unintentionally tells the client that your internal chaos took priority over their needs.

Here are better ways to phrase it, depending on your relationship with the client:

---

### 1. Professional & Standard (Best for most situations)
> *"Thank you for your patience while I got back to you."*  
*(Shifting from "Sorry for the delay" to "Thank you for your patience" subtly frames the interaction positively rather than drawing attention to a mistake.)*

### 2. Friendly & Casual (If you have an established, warm relationship)
> *"Thanks for bearing with me—it’s been quite a busy stretch on our end."*

### 3. Clear & Business-Focused (If you need to acknowledge a long delay)
> *"Apologies for the delayed response, we've had a higher volume of requests than usual this week. Regarding your question..."*

---

### Rule of thumb:
* **Replace:** "Crazy" $\rightarrow$ "Busy", "A high volume of work", or "Full schedule".
* **Focus on the client:** Move straight into solving their problem or answering their question right after the opening line.
