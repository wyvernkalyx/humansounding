To be completely honest: **it is safe, but it is also one of the most common clichés in business copywriting.** 

Almost every company describes themselves as a *"team of passionate experts,"* which means the phrase has lost its meaning to readers. Furthermore, starting with *"Founded in 2019"* leads with dry corporate logistics rather than a compelling reason for the visitor to care.

Here is a breakdown of why you should change it, followed by a few ways to rewrite it.

---

### The Three Main Issues:

1. **It’s about you, not the customer:** A great About Page should actually make the *customer* feel understood first.
2. **"Passionate experts" lacks proof:** *Show* your expertise through what you’ve built or how you think, rather than just claiming it.
3. **The hook is weak:** The first sentence needs to grab attention or explain the core problem you solve.

---

### 3 Ways to Rewrite It (Depending on your style):

#### Option 1: Lead with your "Why" (The Origin Story)
Instead of just saying *when* you started, explain *why* you started.
> *“In 2019, we realized that [Industry/Niche] was broken because of [Common Problem]. We started [Company Name] to fix that.”*

#### Option 2: Lead with the Customer Value (Punchy & Direct)
Tell the visitor immediately what you do for them.
> *“We help [target audience] achieve [specific result]—without the [common headache/frustration].”*

#### Option 3: Conversational & Human
If you want to keep the team aspect, make it sound like real people talking.
> *“We’re a group of [specific role, e.g., designers, engineers, marketers] obsessed with helping [target audience] [achieve goal].”*

---

### A quick exercise to find your new opener:
Answer these two questions:
1. **What specific problem were you trying to solve when you launched in 2019?**
2. **What can your clients do today because your company exists?**

Put the answer to either of those in your opening sentence instead. You can always mention the founding year and your team's background further down the page!
