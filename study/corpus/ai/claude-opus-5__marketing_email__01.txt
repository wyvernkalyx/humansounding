**See your whole team in one screen**

Workload View shows every teammate as a row and every day, week, or month as a column. Assigned hours stack up visually, so overallocation isn't something you discover — it's something you spot in two seconds.

- **Color-coded capacity.** Green means healthy, amber means near capacity, red means someone's booked for 52 hours next week and nobody noticed.
- **Drag to rebalance.** Move a task from an overloaded teammate to someone with room. Dates, dependencies, and notifications update automatically.
- **Real availability, not theoretical.** Workload View accounts for part-time schedules, approved time off, and holidays — so a 3-day week is treated like a 3-day week.
- **Filter by project, team, or skill.** Planning a launch? Look at just those people. Staffing a new client? Find who's actually free.

**Plan forward, not backward**

Toggle on **Forecast Mode** to see the next 90 days, including work that's scheduled but not yet started. It's the difference between reacting to a crunch and preventing one — and it makes resourcing conversations with leadership a lot shorter.
