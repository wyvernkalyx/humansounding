---

**Tips for customizing:**

1. **Replace every bracket** with real numbers. Vague claims ("improved efficiency") are forgettable; "reduced cost per unit by 14%" is not.
2. **Mirror the job posting's language.** If they say "network optimization" or "3PL management," use those exact phrases.
3. **Make the "why this company" paragraph specific.** Reference a recent expansion, acquisition, sustainability initiative, or facility opening—one line of genuine research outperforms three paragraphs of flattery.
4. **Keep it to one page.** Hiring managers in logistics skim; bullets help them find your numbers fast.
