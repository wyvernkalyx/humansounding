**Re: Daniel Okafor — Candidacy for Manager, Data & Insights**

Dear Members of the Committee,

I am writing to offer my strongest recommendation for Daniel Okafor's promotion to Manager, Data & Insights. Daniel has reported to me for the past three years, first as an Analyst and, since early 2023, as a Senior Analyst. In that time he has consistently performed above his level, and I believe this promotion formalizes responsibilities he has already been carrying with distinction.

Daniel's technical contributions speak for themselves. When our attribution reporting broke down after last year's platform migration, he volunteered to rebuild the model from scratch. He spent six weeks reconciling data across four systems, documented every assumption, and delivered a framework that cut monthly reporting time from eleven days to three. That model is now the source of truth for a $40 million media budget, and two other regions have adopted it. More impressive than the output was the process: Daniel proactively brought in colleagues from Finance and IT rather than solving it in isolation, which is precisely the instinct a manager needs.

What distinguishes Daniel, however, is his judgment about people and priorities. He has informally mentored three junior analysts, two of whom have specifically cited his coaching in their own development reviews. He gives feedback directly but without ego, and he is equally good at receiving it. When I pushed back on the scope of his Q3 forecasting proposal, he didn't defend it reflexively; he came back a week later with a narrower, better version and a clear rationale for what he had cut. That combination of conviction and flexibility is rare at any level.

Daniel is also a genuinely effective communicator with non-technical stakeholders. He has presented to the executive team on four occasions, and I have watched him translate genuinely complicated methodology into plain language without oversimplifying or condescending. Our CFO now asks for him by name.

In the interest of a balanced assessment: Daniel's growth area has been delegation. His first instinct is still to take difficult work onto his own plate, particularly under deadline pressure. He and I have discussed this openly, and I have seen real movement over the past two quarters — most notably during the annual planning cycle, when he assigned two significant workstreams to junior colleagues and coached them through rather than absorbing the work himself. He is aware of the tendency and is actively managing it, which is the best predictor I know of continued improvement.

I would add that Daniel has been offered outside opportunities at least twice to my knowledge and has chosen to stay. He is invested in this organization and in the people on this team, and promoting him sends a clear signal about how we reward that.

I recommend Daniel without reservation and would be glad to discuss his candidacy further. Please contact me at the number above.

Sincerely,

Priya Raman
Director of Marketing Operations
