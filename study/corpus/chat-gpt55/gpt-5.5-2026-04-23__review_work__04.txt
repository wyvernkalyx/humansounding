I’d revise it before sending. “Things have been crazy here” can sound a bit informal and may make the delay feel less client-focused.

Better options:

**Simple and professional:**
> Sorry for the delay, and thank you for your patience. Please see my response below.

**If you want to acknowledge it without overexplaining:**
> Apologies for the delay — I appreciate your patience. Things have been busy on our end, but I wanted to follow up with you now.

**If the delay caused an issue:**
> Apologies for the delay, and thank you for your patience. I understand the timing is important, and I appreciate your understanding.

I’d probably send:  
> Apologies for the delay, and thank you for your patience.  

Then move directly into the actual update or answer.
