It’s clear, but it sounds very generic. Phrases like “founded in 2019” and “passionate experts” are common on About pages and don’t immediately tell visitors why they should care.

A stronger opening would lead with what you do, who you help, and what makes you credible or different.

For example:

> We help [type of customer] achieve [specific result] through [your service/product/approach].

Or:

> Since 2019, we’ve helped [audience] solve [problem] with [specific expertise or method].

Or more polished:

> We’re a team of [specialists] helping [customers] [achieve outcome] without [common pain point].

If you want to keep the founding date, make it support the story rather than lead the sentence:

> Since 2019, we’ve been helping [audience] [specific outcome], combining [expertise] with [distinctive approach].

“Passionate experts” is the part I’d most strongly replace. It’s vague. Show the expertise instead: mention your niche, years of experience, results, clients served, methodology, or mission.
