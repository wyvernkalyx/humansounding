It’s **okay, but not ideal**.

**“Quick question about your account”** is short and curiosity-driven, but it can also feel **vague, generic, or even phishing-like**, especially because “your account” is commonly used in spam/scam emails.

Better options depend on the context:

- **If it’s customer support:**  
  “Question about your [Company] account”  
  “Can you confirm one detail on your account?”

- **If it’s sales/outreach:**  
  “Quick question about [Company Name]”  
  “Question about your current [tool/process]”

- **If it’s account-related and important:**  
  “Action needed: Update your [Company] account details”  
  “Please review your [Company] account information”

- **If you want it to feel more personal:**  
  “Quick question, [Name]”  
  “Question about your [specific topic]”

Best general improvement:  
**“Quick question about your [Company/Product] account”**

It’s still concise, but more specific and trustworthy.
