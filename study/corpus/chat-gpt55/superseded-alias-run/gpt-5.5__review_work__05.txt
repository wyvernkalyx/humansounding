It’s clear, but it sounds very generic—phrases like “team of passionate experts” are overused and don’t tell visitors much about what makes you different.

A stronger opening should answer one or more of these:

- What do you do?
- Who do you help?
- What problem do you solve?
- Why should someone trust or care about you?
- What’s distinctive about your approach?

For example, instead of:

> Founded in 2019, we are a team of passionate experts.

You could try:

> Since 2019, we’ve helped [type of customer] solve [specific problem] through [your service/product/approach].

Or:

> We started in 2019 with a simple goal: to make [specific outcome] easier for [specific audience].

Or, if you want something warmer:

> Since 2019, we’ve been building [what you build/do] for people who care about [value/result].

If you share what your company does and your audience, I can help rewrite it into something sharper and more memorable.
