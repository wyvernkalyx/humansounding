Read it as a **hypothesis, not a guarantee**: “Under some conditions, for some customers, using our tool reduced some definition of onboarding time by about 50%.”

Before accepting it, clarify the following:

1. **What does “onboarding time” mean?**  
   - Time from signed offer to first productive day?  
   - Time to complete HR paperwork?  
   - Time until the employee reaches full productivity?  
   - Time for IT/account provisioning?

2. **Half compared to what baseline?**  
   If your current onboarding takes 10 days, are they claiming 5 days? If it takes 3 days, are they claiming 1.5? The value depends heavily on your starting point.

3. **Is it average, median, or best case?**  
   “Cut in half” may refer to a cherry-picked customer, a pilot group, or a subset of the process.

4. **Which parts does the tool actually affect?**  
   A tool might reduce paperwork time by 50%, but if paperwork is only 20% of onboarding, total onboarding may only improve by 10%.

5. **What assumptions are required?**  
   The claim may depend on clean HR data, integrations, manager participation, prebuilt workflows, or process changes your team has not implemented yet.

6. **What evidence supports it?**  
   Ask for case studies, benchmarks, before/after metrics, and references from companies similar to yours in size, industry, and complexity.

7. **What is the implementation cost and ramp time?**  
   A tool that saves time later may initially require process redesign, integrations, training, and change management.

8. **What metric should you use in a pilot?**  
   Define success upfront: for example, “reduce average time from offer acceptance to completed provisioning from 8 business days to 4.”

A practical way to read the claim is:

> “They believe they can materially reduce some onboarding bottlenecks, but we need to validate whether that translates into a 50% reduction in our end-to-end onboarding process.”

Ask them: **“Show us exactly where the 50% comes from, what baseline you used, and how we would measure it in a 60–90 day pilot.”**
