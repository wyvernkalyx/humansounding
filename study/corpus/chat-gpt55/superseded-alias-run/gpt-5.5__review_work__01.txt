It’s not bad, but it’s very generic.

**What works:**
- It sounds positive and professional.
- It communicates that the company helps businesses improve.
- It’s broad enough to fit many industries.

**What doesn’t work:**
- Phrases like **“empower,” “unlock their full potential,”** and **“innovative solutions”** are overused.
- It doesn’t say what you actually do, who you help, or what specific value you provide.
- It could belong to almost any consulting, software, marketing, or tech company.

A stronger version would be more specific. For example:

**If you’re a software company:**  
> Our mission is to help small businesses save time and grow faster through simple, reliable software.

**If you’re a consulting firm:**  
> Our mission is to help businesses improve operations, solve complex challenges, and achieve measurable growth.

**If you want something polished but still broad:**  
> Our mission is to help businesses grow by delivering practical, innovative solutions that create lasting value.

So: **it’s usable, but not especially memorable.** It would be much stronger if it included what kind of businesses you serve and what outcomes you help them achieve.
