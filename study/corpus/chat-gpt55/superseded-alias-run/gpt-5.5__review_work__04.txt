I’d revise it before sending. “Things have been crazy here” can sound vague or unprofessional, and it shifts focus away from the client.

Better options:

**Simple and professional:**
> Apologies for the delay, and thank you for your patience. I’ve now [attached/reviewed/completed] the [item].

**If you need to explain briefly:**
> Apologies for the delay. I’ve had a few competing priorities this week, but I appreciate your patience and wanted to follow up with [update].

**If the delay caused inconvenience:**
> I’m sorry for the delay and any inconvenience this caused. Thank you for your patience — here is [the update/the document/my response].

Best version depends on what you’re sending, but I’d avoid “things have been crazy here.”
