It’s **okay, but not ideal**.

**“Quick question about your account”** is clear enough, but it can feel a bit generic or even slightly phishing-like because it doesn’t say which account or why you’re reaching out.

Better options would be more specific:

- **Question about your [Company/Product] account**
- **Quick question about your [Product] setup**
- **Can you confirm a detail on your [Company] account?**
- **Following up on your [Company] account**
- **Need your input on your [Product] account**

If this is for sales or customer support, I’d avoid making it sound urgent or mysterious. A more specific subject line will likely feel more trustworthy.
