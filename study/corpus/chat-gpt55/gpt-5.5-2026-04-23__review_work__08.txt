Straight: **“Synergize” is weak as a product name.**

Why:

- **It sounds like corporate buzzword bingo.** “Synergy/synergize” has been overused for years in business decks and consultant-speak.
- **It’s generic.** It doesn’t tell me what the product does, who it’s for, or why I should care.
- **It may feel dated.** It has a 2000s enterprise-software vibe.
- **SEO/trademark will likely be hard.** The word is common, and many companies/products probably use variants of it.
- **It doesn’t create much emotional pull.** It sounds functional and abstract, not memorable or ownable.

When it could work:

- If your product is deliberately aimed at **enterprise collaboration, integrations, ops, or workflow automation**, it at least fits the category.
- If the brand voice is serious, corporate, and B2B, it’s not disastrous.
- If paired with a strong descriptor — e.g. **Synergize CRM**, **Synergize Ops**, **Synergize AI** — it becomes clearer, though still generic.

My honest take: **I’d rename it if you still can.** Aim for something more specific, distinctive, and less buzzwordy. “Synergize” sounds like a feature benefit, not a brand.
